Guide

Start your journey
in three steps.

01

Creating An Account

  1. Personal Info

    Enter your name, contact details, birthdate, and address → Click Continue

  2. Medical Details

    Select your blood type and add health info → Click Continue

  3. Account Setup

    Create and confirm your password → Click Create Account

02

Logging In

  1. Enter Email

    Type your registered email address

  2. Enter Password

    Type your account password

  3. Click Login

    Access your account

Optional:

  • Click Forgot Password? if you need to reset it
  • Select Register Now if you don't have an account yet
03

Donating

  1. Provide Details

    Enter your blood type, availability, and location

  2. Add Medical Info

    Include any required health details

  3. Confirm Submission

    Review your information and submit

  4. Get Matched

    Wait to be contacted when a recipient needs your blood
