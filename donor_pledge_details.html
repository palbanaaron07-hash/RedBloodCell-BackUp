Official Blood Donation & Medical Screening Instructions

Step-by-step guidance for a safe, smooth, and comfortable blood donation

1
Phase 1: Donor Registration

Admissions & Questionnaire

  • Valid ID Required: Bring 1 government-issued photo ID (Driver's License, PhilHealth, UMID, Passport, National ID, School/Company ID).
  • Donor History Form: Complete the standard Department of Health (DOH) confidential health questionnaire honestly.
  • Pass Verification: Present your BloodConnect Donation Pass reference code to priority triage.
2
Phase 2: Physical & Clinical Exam

Vital Signs & Hemoglobin Screening

  • Vital Signs Check: Blood pressure must be within 90/60 – 140/90 mmHg; Pulse rate 60–100 bpm regular; Body temp under 37.5°C.
  • Hemoglobin Fingerstick: A quick micro-drop test. Hemoglobin must be at least 12.5 g/dL (for females) or 13.0 g/dL (for males) to guarantee donor safety.
  • Medical Officer Consultation: A doctor or licensed med-tech reviews medical history and grants clearance.
3
Phase 3: Blood Collection (Phlebotomy)

Safe & Sterile Blood Extraction (10 - 15 Mins)

  • 100% Sterile Equipment: Extraction uses a brand-new, single-use, sterile needle and blood bag. There is zero risk of catching infections from donating.
  • Volume Collected: Approx. 450 mL (1 unit), which represents less than 8-10% of your total blood volume and is quickly replenished by your body.
  • Comfort & Relaxation: Relax in the donor recliner, squeeze the soft stress ball gently, and breathe normally.
4
Phase 4: Post-Donation Recovery & Perks

Rest, Refreshments & Assistance Handover

  • Mandatory 15-Minute Rest: Rest comfortably in the recovery lounge with feet elevated.
  • Enjoy Your Meal & Drinks: Consume the iron-rich food and sweet refreshments provided by the recipient's family.
  • Allowance & Handover: Receive your travel allowance reimbursement and token of appreciation.

BEFORE & AFTER DONATION — WHAT TO DO

  • Hydrate Well: Drink at least 3 to 4 glasses (500-750 mL) of water 2-3 hours before donating.
  • Eat a Healthy Meal: Have an iron-rich meal (e.g. lean meat, fish, beans, green leafy vegetables) 1-2 hours before.
  • Get Adequate Sleep: Have at least 6 to 8 hours of uninterrupted rest the night before.
  • Wear Comfortable Clothing: Wear shirts with sleeves that can easily roll up past the elbow.
  • Keep Bandage On: Keep the venipuncture bandage clean and dry for at least 4-6 hours.

THINGS TO AVOID

  • No Alcohol: Avoid all beer, wine, and liquor for at least 24 hours prior to donation.
  • Avoid Fatty Foods: Do not eat excessively greasy or deep-fried food on donation day as it may affect plasma clarity.
  • No Strenuous Exercise: Avoid heavy lifting, intense gym workouts, or running for 24 hours after extraction.
  • Avoid Smoking: Refrain from smoking for at least 2 hours before and after donation.
  • No Aspirin: Avoid taking aspirin or blood thinners within 48 hours prior if donating platelets.

Mandatory Laboratory Screening Tests Performed

Every unit of collected blood undergoes rigorous clinical testing before transfusion for patient and public safety
